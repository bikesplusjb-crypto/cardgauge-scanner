Best results: 3–10 cards, flat, not overlapping, camera roughly overhead, even lighting. After the fronts are detected, you can flip the cards in the same positions and capture the backs.
⚠ Shiny/refractor/prizm cards are harder to detect reliably than plain cards — direct glare can cause one to be missed or cropped wrong. Angling the card slightly to avoid a bright reflection, or using softer/indirect light, helps. Set the expected card count below so a miss gets flagged instead of slipping through.
⚠ Don't fill the whole frame with one card — leave a visible margin of background around every edge, even scanning just one. A card pressed up to the edge of the photo can get cropped wrong, since the detector needs to see all four edges to find the card.
